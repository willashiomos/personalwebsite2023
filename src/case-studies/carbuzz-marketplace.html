Case Study

Carbuzz Marketplace

UI Design | Data Visualization | Development | Branding

CarBuzz Marketplace is a car buying platform built for readers who already trust CarBuzz for reviews and research. The project redesigned discovery, added listings, and vehicle detail into a single system to create the ease and clarity shoppers need to decide with confidence.

Challenge

Car shopping is noisy. Listings bury value under filters. Prices are hard to judge without context. The challenge was to design a marketplace that could do many things at once: help someone articulate what they want, show whether a price is actually good, and keep CarBuzz’s editorial authority intact from homepage to vehicle page.

Screens

Homepage

Opens the experience with the search and categories of listings so discovery can happen through many paths.

Results page

Allows the user to search by AI prompt or filters. Every listing still answers the same question: what is it, what does it cost, and is that price any good?

Article inserts

Marketplace listings carousel and price trends vignettes are inserted within articles and direct users to the marketplace.

Campaigns

Designed and developed multiple ad placements to direct users to the marketplace.
